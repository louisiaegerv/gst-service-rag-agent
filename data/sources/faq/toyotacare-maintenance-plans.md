## ToyotaCare

No-cost maintenance plan included with the purchase or lease of every new Toyota, covering normal factory-scheduled maintenance.

- **Standard coverage:** 2 years or 25,000 miles, whichever comes first.
- **24-hour Roadside Assistance** included for 3 years, unlimited miles.
- Some models have adjusted terms:
  - **bZ, bZ Woodland, bZ4X:** ToyotaCare for 2 years or 25,000 miles, plus 3 years/unlimited miles Roadside Assistance.
  - **Mirai:** enhanced to 3 years or 35,000 miles of no-cost maintenance, plus 3 years of Roadside Assistance.

## ToyotaCare Plus

Extended protection plan purchased once ToyotaCare expires. Coverage starts once ToyotaCare expires, with first service available at 15,000 miles.

- **Coverage:** up to 5 years or 75,000 miles from the vehicle's date of first use (whichever comes first). Date of first use = when the vehicle is first put into service.
- Some contexts describe it as "up to 6 years or 13 services" — check the plan details for the specific vehicle, as terms vary by model/program.

## ToyotaCare Plus Service Drive

A variant of ToyotaCare Plus for owned vehicles (including EVs).

- **Coverage:** up to 4 years or 45,000 miles from date of first use, whichever comes first.

## Toyota Service Care

Prepaid maintenance plan with Roadside Assistance, purchased separately (not tied to the free ToyotaCare period).

- **Coverage:** 2 years, unlimited miles.
- Coverage starts immediately once purchased.
- Can be renewed repeatedly.
